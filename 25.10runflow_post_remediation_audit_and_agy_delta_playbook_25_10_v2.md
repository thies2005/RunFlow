2. **2 High-Severity Residual Bypasses Remain in `AGY-01` and `AGY-11` (`F-01-R1` and `F-12-R1`)**:
   - **`F-01-R1` (High — `AGY-01` Residual Flaw in `Web/src/app/api/admin/backups/upload/route.ts:118-134`)**: `zlib.gunzipSync(buffer, { maxOutputLength: 65536 })` is called on the entire compressed `.sql.gz` buffer. Additionally, `getCsrfSigningSecret()` at line 14 falls back to `'runflow-csrf-dev-secret'` without failing closed in production.
3. **1 Medium-Severity State Bug in Newly Added Endpoint (`F-14` in `DELETE /api/mobile/v1/activities/[id]`)**:
